Brief. Intake. Vault. Retrieve.

Four phases, each one documented. You always know where your firearm is, who handled it, and what it takes to get it back.

01 · Brief

Private consult

A short, confidential conversation to understand what you're storing, for how long, and how you'd like to be contacted. Nothing more than we need.

02 · Intake

Documented intake

By appointment. Each firearm is inspected, tagged, photographed for condition, and logged in our FFL records. You receive a written storage receipt.

03 · Vault

Secure storage

Firearms are held in a locked, access-controlled vault, cased and individually identified. Access is limited to authorized MKV personnel.

04 · Retrieve

Scheduled return

When you're ready, schedule a pickup. We verify identity and complete the transfer steps the law requires, then the firearm goes home with you.

Frequently asked.

Short answers here. Anything specific to your situation, ask us directly.

Do I have to explain why I'm storing a firearm?

No. Your reason is yours. We need the information required to identify you and the firearm and to keep accurate records. Your personal circumstances aren't part of that.

Who can see my information?

Access is limited to authorized MKV personnel. As a licensed dealer, we are required to keep certain firearm records and make them available to federal authorities as the law provides. We don't share your information beyond that or what the law requires.

What do I need to bring to intake?

A valid government-issued photo ID, the firearm unloaded and cased, and any accessories you want stored with it. We'll confirm the full checklist when we schedule your appointment.

How do I get my firearm back?

Contact us to schedule a pickup. We'll verify your identity and complete any transfer paperwork and background check required under federal and applicable state law before release. If you're not able to pass a required check, we'll walk you through your options.

What can you store?
